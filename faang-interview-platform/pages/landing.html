FAQ
Common questions
Is my camera or microphone data stored anywhere? ▾
No. Camera video is displayed locally in your browser and never transmitted. Microphone audio is processed by the browser's built-in Web Speech API — only the final text transcript is sent to the AI. We store no audio or video.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? ▾
Yes. Your subscription remains active until the end of the current 30-day period. After that it won't renew. No hidden fees, no questions asked.
Can I add my own questions to the platform? ▾
Yes — switch to Interviewer Mode to add LeetCode problems by URL, custom DSA questions with test cases, HLD/LLD design questions with expected coverage, behavioural questions with probes, and AI knowledge topics with subtopics.
Which companies' interview styles does the AI simulate? ▾
Amazon, Google, Meta/Facebook, Flipkart, and DeepMind — each with distinct interviewer personas and probing styles specific to that company's known interview patterns.
Does it work on mobile? ▾
The platform is designed for desktop/laptop use — coding rounds need a full keyboard. The AI Knowledge and Behavioural rounds work on mobile.
